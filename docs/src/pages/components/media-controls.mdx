# MediaControls

<Lead>
    Transport controls for any media source: play/pause, seek bar, time, mute and volume, plus
    optional previous/next and fullscreen. They only report what the user does, so they drive a
    video, an audio file, a YouTube player or a speaker in another room alike.
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="MediaControls" />

## Basic

MediaControls is fully controlled: pass `playing`, `currentTime`, `duration`, `volume` and `muted`
from your player and handle the callbacks. `onSeek` fires once when a drag on the seek bar ends, so
a player is not flooded with seeks while dragging; the time readout follows the drag meanwhile.
Buttons for previous, next and fullscreen appear when you pass their handler.

## Live and minimal

A `duration` of `0`, `NaN` or `Infinity` means unknown or live: the seek bar is disabled and `LIVE`
replaces the total time. Without `onVolumeChange` and `onMutedChange` the volume controls are
hidden.

<Demo name="media-controls/Live" />

For a ready-made player use [MediaPlayer](/components/media-player) (video and audio files) or
[YouTubePlayer](/components/you-tube-player).

## Sound

The buttons play `hover_button` and `click`; dragging a slider plays `drag_start` and `drag_end`,
through the nearest `SfxProvider`. See [Sound effects](/getting-started/sound).

## Accessibility

- The controls are a `role="group"` named **Media controls** (change it with `aria-label`). The
  buttons are named **Play** / **Pause**, **Mute** / **Unmute** (with `aria-pressed`),
  **Previous**, **Next** and **Fullscreen**; the sliders **Seek** (with the time as
  `aria-valuetext`) and **Volume**.
- Shortcuts while focus is inside the controls: `K` plays or pauses, `M` mutes, ← and → seek 5
  seconds. Keys a focused slider uses itself are left to it.
