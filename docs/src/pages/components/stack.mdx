# Stack

<Lead>
    Lays out its children in a column or a row with spacing from the design tokens. It covers most
    layout needs without Tailwind, and it is the layout building block for generated windows.
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="Stack" />

## Column

By default the children stack top to bottom and stretch to the full width. `gap` takes a spacing
step: `xs` (4px), `sm` (8px), `md` (12px, the default), `lg` (24px), `xl` (32px) or `none`. The
steps read the `--s-*` tokens, so they follow a theme.

<Demo name="stack/Column" align="start" />

## Row

`direction="row"` lays the children out left to right. `align` sets the cross-axis alignment,
`justify` the distribution along the row, and `wrap` lets them flow onto further lines. Stacks nest.

<Demo name="stack/Row" align="stretch" />

## Accessibility

Stack renders a plain `<div>` and adds no semantics. Pass `role` or `aria-*` attributes when the group
needs them; they reach the root element, and so does the `ref`.
