# CornerBrackets

<Lead>
    CornerBrackets draws four small L-shaped marks at the corners of its content, the signature
    frame of the HUD. Wrap any box in it to give it the same targeting look as a
    [Panel](/components/panel).
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="CornerBrackets" />

## Basic

CornerBrackets renders a `relative` wrapper `<div>` around its children and places the brackets 2px
outside the wrapper's edges. Padding on `className` moves the brackets away from the content.

<Demo name="corner-brackets/Basic" />

## Focused

`focused` brightens the brackets to `--accent-bright` at full opacity. The change fades over 200ms,
so it works well for hover, selection or an active state you keep in your own state.

<Demo name="corner-brackets/Focused" />

## Sizes

`size` sets the length of each bracket arm in pixels. The default is 12.

<Demo name="corner-brackets/Sizes" />

## Accessibility

- The four brackets are `aria-hidden` and ignore pointer events; only the children are exposed to
  assistive technology.
- The wrapper is a plain `<div>` without a role. `focused` is purely visual and does not move
  keyboard focus.
- The only motion is a 200ms color and opacity transition when `focused` changes; with
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the change is instant.
