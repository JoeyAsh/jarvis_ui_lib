# Reactor

<Lead>
    Reactor is a large, soft hemisphere of accent light rising from the bottom of the screen, with a
    thin arc at its rim. It gives the space below the orb an ambient glow.
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="Reactor" />

## Basic

Reactor is a 900px circle, `position: fixed`, centered horizontally and pushed 360px below the
bottom edge, with `z-index: 0`. Render it once near the root of your app, after the backdrop, and
give content on top a position. The size is fixed and tuned for full-height viewports; in the
shorter demo frame only the lower part of the glow is visible.

<Demo name="reactor/Basic" height="tall" />

`HUDShell` renders a Reactor by default (`reactor`). See
[Scene](/components/scene) for a full backdrop that layers Reactor on top of the scene.

## Accessibility

- The glow is `aria-hidden`, ignores pointer events and does not animate. It is tinted from
  `--accent`.
