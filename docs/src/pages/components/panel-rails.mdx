# PanelRails

<Lead>
    PanelRails adds two dashed vertical rails just outside the left and right edges of a surface.
    Use them as an extra highlight for a hovered or selected panel.
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="PanelRails" />

## Visible

PanelRails covers its parent (`position: absolute; inset: 0`), so the parent needs `relative` and
must not clip overflow: the rails sit 4px outside its edges. They start 26px below the top, leaving
room for a header strip, and end 4px above the bottom. `visible` fades them in over 300ms.

<Demo name="panel-rails/Basic" />

To combine PanelRails with the other chrome layers, see the custom card on
[LightTrace](/components/light-trace).

## Accessibility

- The rails are `aria-hidden` and ignore pointer events. They are a purely visual cue.
- The only motion is the 300ms opacity transition when `visible` changes.
