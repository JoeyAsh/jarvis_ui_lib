# Scene

<Lead>
    Scene is the full-screen backdrop of the HUD: a dark radial background with a drifting grid,
    scanlines, stars, a vignette, film grain and a horizon glow. Render it once behind everything
    else.
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="Scene" />

## Basic

Scene is a `fixed`, full-viewport layer with `z-index: 0` and an opaque background. Content that
should appear above it needs a position (e.g. `relative`) and must come after it in the tree. In
this demo the frame contains the layer; in an app it covers the viewport.

<Demo name="scene/Basic" height="tall" />

## Layers

`grid`, `stars` and `scanlines` switch the optional layers off; all three are on by default. The
background, vignette, grain and horizon glow are always drawn.

<Demo name="scene/Layers" height="tall" />

## Backdrop

Combine Scene with a [Reactor](/components/reactor) and [ViewportCorners](/components/viewport-corners)
for the complete HUD backdrop, then put your panels on top. `HUDShell`
renders exactly this stack for you, with props to toggle each piece.

<Demo name="scene/Backdrop" height="tall" />

## Accessibility

- The whole backdrop is `aria-hidden`. Its layers ignore pointer events.
- The grid drift and the star twinkle run continuously and do not respond to
  `prefers-reduced-motion`. Pass `grid={false}` and `stars={false}` for a static backdrop.
