# Tooltip

<Lead>
    A small text bubble that explains its trigger on hover or keyboard focus. It is the standard
    companion of [IconButton](/components/icon-button).
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="Tooltip" />

## Basic

Wrap a single focusable element. The tooltip opens after `delayMs` (300ms by default) on hover and
immediately on keyboard focus.

<Demo name="tooltip/Basic" />

## Placements

`placement` puts the bubble on the `top` (default), `bottom`, `left` or `right` of the trigger.
The bubble does not flip when it runs out of space, so choose a side with room.

<Demo name="tooltip/Placements" />

## Toolbar

A row of icon buttons with tooltips below them. `delayMs={0}` opens them instantly while the
pointer sweeps across the toolbar.

<Demo name="tooltip/Toolbar" />

<Callout variant="info" title="Positioning">
    The bubble is absolutely positioned next to the trigger, not portaled. Ancestors with `overflow:
    hidden` clip it.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- The bubble has `role="tooltip"` and an id; while it is open the trigger gets `aria-describedby`
  pointing at it.
- It opens on focus and closes on blur and on Escape.
- Tooltip content is a supplementary description. Do not put interactive content in it, and do not
  rely on it as the only accessible name of the trigger (give an `IconButton` its `label`).
- `disabled` keeps the tooltip mounted but never shows it.
