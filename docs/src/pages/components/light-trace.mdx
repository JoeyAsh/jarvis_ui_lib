# LightTrace

<Lead>
    LightTrace sends four short strips of light around the border of its parent. It marks a surface
    as live or active.
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="LightTrace" />

## Basic

LightTrace is absolutely positioned 1px outside its parent's padding box, so it runs over a 1px
border. Give the parent `position: relative` (the `relative` utility). One full lap takes six
seconds.

<Demo name="light-trace/Basic" />

## Color

`color` takes any CSS color. It tints the tails of the strips, and the bright center and its glow
become a lightened mix of the same color, so the whole trace follows it. Without `color` the tails
use `--accent` and the center `--accent-bright`. Prefer theme variables such as `var(--warning)`.

<Demo name="light-trace/Color" />

## Building a custom card

LightTrace, [PanelBloom](/components/panel-bloom), [PanelRails](/components/panel-rails) and
[CornerBrackets](/components/corner-brackets) are the layers a [Panel](/components/panel) is made of.
Combine them to give your own surfaces the same chrome. Put the layers first and give the content
`relative` so it paints above them.

<Demo name="light-trace/CustomCard" />

## Accessibility

- The trace is `aria-hidden` and ignores pointer events.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the strips are not animated and not drawn; the trace is
  hidden.
