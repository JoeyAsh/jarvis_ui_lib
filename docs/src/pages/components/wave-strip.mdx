# WaveStrip

<Lead>
    A compact, seven-bar waveform that sits inline with text, for example next to a "now playing"
    caption. It is the small sibling of [WaveformMeter](/components/waveform-meter).
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="WaveStrip" />

## Inline with text

WaveStrip renders a 12px tall `inline-flex` `<span>`, so it lines up with small caption text. Put a
`mirrored` strip on the other side of the text for a symmetric look.

<Demo name="wave-strip/NowPlaying" />

## Paused

With `active={false}` the bars collapse to dim 2px stubs and stop moving. Toggle it with your
playback state.

<Demo name="wave-strip/Paused" />

## Accessibility

- The strip is `aria-hidden`; the text next to it must carry the meaning (for example "Playing" or
  "Paused").
- It has no interactive behavior and plays no sound.
