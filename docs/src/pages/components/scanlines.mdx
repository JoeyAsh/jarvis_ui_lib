# Scanlines

<Lead>
    Scanlines lays fine horizontal CRT lines over its content. Use it to give a readout, a metric or
    a media tile the look of a HUD display.
</Lead>

<ComponentMeta component="Scanlines" />

## Basic

Scanlines wraps its children in a `relative`, `overflow-hidden` `<div>` and draws the lines on top
with a screen blend, so the content stays readable. Style the wrapper with `className`.

<Demo name="scanlines/Basic" />

## Sweep

`sweep` adds a light band that moves across the content in a loop, e.g. while data is loading or
streaming in.

<Demo name="scanlines/Sweep" />

For a full-screen scanline layer use [Scene](/components/scene), which includes one.

## Accessibility

- The scanline overlay and the sweep band are `aria-hidden` and ignore pointer events; the children
  stay fully accessible and clickable.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` both the scanlines and the sweep band are hidden
  (`motion-reduce:hidden`), and only the content remains.
